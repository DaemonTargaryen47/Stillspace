## 💭 Why Stillspace Exists

Every social platform is built to make you perform — post more, respond faster, stay visible, keep the streak alive. That works for some people. It quietly exhausts others.

Stillspace is for the second group. It's not social media, and it's not a chat app. It's built for people — often avoidant, often just tired — who want to **signal that they exist and that they're okay (or not) without triggering a conversation, a notification, or an expectation.**

You can say "I need space today" and nobody will ask why. You can go quiet for a week and nothing will look broken when you come back.

---

## 🧭 Product Philosophy

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Zero Social Competition** | No likes, no follower counts, no streaks, no public profiles |
| **Zero Urgency** | No red badges, no typing indicators, no read receipts, no "last seen" |
| **Total Autonomy** | Share your state, react silently, or disappear completely — always your call |
| **Calm Aesthetics** | High whitespace, muted tones, gentle animation, nothing that demands attention |

## 🌿 Guest Mode — try it in 10 seconds, no signup

Everything below works instantly, with no email or password:

- **Anonymous Global Feed** — browse quiet, identity-free whispers from strangers ("Someone is taking space today," "Someone is quiet but present")
- **Emotional Echo** — write a thought, get a gentle reflection back — validation without AI chatter
- **Borrowed Presence Reactions** — send anonymous, non-binding micro-reactions to global whispers
- **Shadow Circle Preview** — experience a simulated 10-person circle with soft real-time status updates
- **Emotional Weather Room** — ambient visual environments that shift with mood: calm, neutral, overwhelmed, quiet
- **Interactive Status Preview** — try the status-selection interface in local demo mode

---

## 🔐 Signed-In Mode — a persistent, still-anonymous space

No email or password — just an instant, privacy-first anonymous ID.

- **Private Circle (max 10 people)** — mutual, consent-based connections via invite link or code
- **One Active Emotional Status** — visible only to your circle: *I'm okay · I need space today · I feel overwhelmed · I'm quiet but present*
- **Quiet Acknowledgements** — low-pressure responses like "seen," "thinking of you," "silent support" — no conversation triggered
- **Controlled, Optional Chat** — only exists if both people opt in; zero read receipts, zero typing indicators, zero urgency
- **Disappear Mode** — go instantly invisible to your entire circle; no trace, no explanation needed
- **Private Status History** — a personal, private log of your past moods, visible only to you
